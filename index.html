WELCOME TO AWA

A stronger community starts with connection.

Albanian Women's Alliance (AWA) began with a simple idea: Albanian women deserve more opportunities to connect, share knowledge, support one another and grow. Whether navigating life in America, finding professional guidance, mentoring the next generation or simply knowing someone is there when help is needed, we believe we can do more together.

United in sisterhood. Empowered to rise.
